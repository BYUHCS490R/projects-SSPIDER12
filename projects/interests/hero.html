Interest Hero

Spider-Man, also known as Peter Parker, is a relatable hero defined by responsibility, empathy, and resilience. He lives by the belief that great power comes with great responsibility.

His humor during battles hides the fear and pressure he carries. Spider-Man is a hero who continues to stand up no matter how many times he falls.

Spider-Man’s Powers
Superhuman strength, agility, reflexes, wall-crawling, and enhanced senses.
Energy Abilities
Some versions of Spider-Man have special energy-based powers.
