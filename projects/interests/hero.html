Spider-Man, often known as Peter Parker, is a deeply relatable hero defined by his dedication to duty, empathy, and resilience. Following the tragic lesson that "with great power comes great responsibility'.

His character shines through his witty, lighthearted banter during fights, which masks the immense pressure and fear he often faces. Spider-Man is not just a hero who wins battles, but one who takes whatever the world gives him and keeps getting back up.
