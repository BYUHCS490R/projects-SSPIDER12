Story Arcs

Marvel and DC have many popular stories. Some show big fights, team-ups, and changes in their worlds.

Marvel Civil War

Heroes disagree about government control, which leads to a fight between Iron Man and Captain America.

Infinity Saga

Thanos collects powerful stones and becomes very strong, so the heroes work together to stop him.

DC Flashpoint

The Flash changes the timeline and creates a different and dangerous version of the world.

Justice League Formation

Some of the strongest heroes come together to form a team and protect the world.
