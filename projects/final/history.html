Important Story Arcs

Marvel and DC have created some of the most iconic superhero storylines, featuring major battles, emotional moments, and universe-changing events.

Marvel Civil War

Heroes split into opposing sides over government control, causing conflict between Iron Man and Captain America.

Infinity Saga

Thanos collects all Infinity Stones and threatens the universe, forcing heroes to unite.

DC Flashpoint

The Flash alters the timeline, creating a dangerous alternate reality.

Justice League Formation

The greatest heroes of Earth unite to form the Justice League and defend the world.
