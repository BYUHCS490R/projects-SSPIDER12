Marvel & DC Heroes

Here are four of the most famous superheroes from Marvel and DC universes.

Spider-Man (Marvel)

Peter Parker is a young hero with spider-like abilities who protects New York City.

Iron Man (Marvel)

Tony Stark is a genius inventor who uses a high-tech suit to fight crime.

Superman (DC)

Superman is an alien with super strength, speed, and flight, protecting Earth.

Batman (DC)

Batman is a billionaire detective who fights crime using intelligence and technology.
