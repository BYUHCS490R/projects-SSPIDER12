What Are Superpowers?

Superpowers are special abilities that superheroes in Marvel and DC use to fight villains, protect people, and save the world. Marvel Characters Source

Super Strength

Super strength allows heroes like Superman to lift massive objects and defeat powerful enemies. DC Superman Info

Advanced Technology

Heroes like Iron Man use advanced suits and technology to gain flight, weapons, and defense systems. Iron Man Source

Super Speed

Speedsters like The Flash can move faster than light and travel through time. Flash Info

Examples of Powers

Spider-Man has spider-like abilities, Hulk has unlimited strength when angry, and Wonder Woman has divine strength and combat skills. Spider-Man Source
