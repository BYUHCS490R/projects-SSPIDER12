What Are Superpowers?

Superpowers are special abilities that superheroes use to fight villains, protect people, and save the world.

Super Strength

Some heroes like Superman have super strength, allowing them to lift heavy objects and fight strong enemies.

Advanced Technology

Iron Man uses a high-tech suit that gives him flight, weapons, and protection in battle.

Super Speed

The Flash can run at extreme speeds, even faster than light, and react almost instantly.

More Examples

Spider-Man has spider-like abilities, Hulk becomes stronger when angry, and Wonder Woman is known for strength and combat skills.
