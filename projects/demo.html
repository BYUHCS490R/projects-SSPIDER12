What Makes a Hero?

SUPERHEROES

Superheroes represent courage, responsibility, and the desire to protect others. Characters like Spider-Man, Batman, Superman, and Ironman inspire people through their strength and moral values. Even though they face powerful enemies, they continue to stand up for justice and protect innocent lives.

Each superhero has a unique story that shapes their personality and choices. Some rely on intelligence and technology, while others possess extraordinary powers from another world. Despite their differences, they all share a commitment to doing what is right, even when it is difficult.
