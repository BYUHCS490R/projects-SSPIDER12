What Makes a Hero?

Superheroes are known for their bravery, strength, and strong moral values. Characters like Spider-Man, Batman, Superman, and Ironman inspire people through their courage and dedication to protecting others. Even when facing powerful enemies, they refuse to give up on justice.

Although each hero has a different background and set of abilities, they all share a commitment to doing what is right. Some rely on intelligence and technology, while others possess extraordinary powers. Their stories remind us that true heroism comes from character and responsibility.
