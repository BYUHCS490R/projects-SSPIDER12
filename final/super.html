What Are Superpowers?

Superpowers are special abilities that superheroes use to protect people and fight villains. Each hero has unique powers that make them strong in different ways.

Physical Powers

These powers improve strength, speed, or durability. Heroes with these abilities are powerful in close combat.

Transformation Powers

These allow heroes to change their form, such as turning into animals or other powerful versions of themselves.

Elemental Powers

These powers allow control over elements like fire, water, electricity, or air.

Examples

Some heroes can stretch their bodies, control fire, freeze enemies with ice, or transform into powerful forms during battle.
